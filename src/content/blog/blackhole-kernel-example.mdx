$$
  \operatorname{RMSNorm}(\mathbf{x})
  =
  \boldsymbol{\gamma} \odot
  \frac{\mathbf{x}}
  {\sqrt{\frac{1}{d}\sum_{i=1}^{d} x_i^2 + \epsilon}}
$$

((Here, d is the feature dimension, γ is the learned scale, and ε ensures numerical stability.))
